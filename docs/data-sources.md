# Data sources

Three jobs for data: **(1) prove the problem**, **(2) power the demo**, **(3) measure progress.**
Rule: every number in the pitch has a source and date. Uncertain items are marked ⚠️ verify.

## 1. Sentiment: staying vs. leaving

### A. What people *say* (published surveys)
| Source | What it gives | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Gensler City Pulse** (2024) | Intent to leave by metro and age; reasons (cost, climate, traffic) | 51.8% of childless 18–34s in Miami likely to leave |
| **Suffolk / WSVN poll** | Miami residents on cost of living, considering leaving FL | 75% call cost unmanageable; 36% considering leaving |
| **Knight / Gallup, Soul of the Community** | What drives attachment to a place (26 cities incl. Miami) | Older (2008–2010) but the foundational study |
| **AmeriCorps Volunteering and Civic Life** (Census CPS supplement) | Volunteering, helping neighbors, civic ties by metro | Miami bottom 3 |
| **Miami Tech Works gap report** (2024) | Miami has tech talent but isn't keeping it | Local, tech-specific |

### B. What people *do* (behavioral migration)
| Source | What it gives | Access |
|---|---|---|
| **Census ACS county-to-county flows** | Where Miami-Dade movers go, by year | Free, census.gov |
| **IRS SOI migration data** | Households and income moving in/out by county | Free, irs.gov |
| **LinkedIn Economic Graph** | Tech talent migration by metro and role | Published reports |
| **U-Haul migration index / USPS change-of-address** | Directional moving signals | Annual reports / FOIA-based news |

### C. What people say *unprompted* (unstructured sentiment)
| Source | How to use it | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Trends** | "moving out of Miami" vs. "moving to Miami" over time | Free; makes a strong pitch chart |
| **Reddit** (r/Miami, relocation and tech career subreddits) | Collect "leaving Miami / moved to Miami" posts; use Claude to classify the *reasons* (community, cost, transit, career, climate) | Follow Reddit's API terms; aggregate only, no usernames |
| **Blind / Glassdoor** | How Miami tech employees talk about the city and offices | Manual review |

> Gap: **no one publishes retention data for Miami tech transplants.** Rooted's check-ins would be the first dataset, and that is a selling point.

### D. Our own primary research (the strongest evidence)
| Method | Goal |
|---|---|
| Interviews with friends who left (15 min) | Real triggers and "what would have kept you" |
| Transplant survey (Google Form via Refresh Miami, LinkedIn, offices) | Baseline using the [5 check-in questions](metrics.md#monthly-check-in-5-questions-asked-by-el-pana) |
| Student conversations (FIU / MDC computing clubs) | Demand for engineer mentors |
| Smoke test signup page | People want it |
| Mini-pilot (one tennis meetup or mentoring session) | It works in real life |

## 2. Data that powers the demo

| Feature | Data | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sports and hobbies | Parks, tennis courts, rec programs | Miami-Dade County Open Data (parks GIS), city parks departments |
| Meetups and rituals | Run clubs, leagues, tech meetups | Refresh Miami calendar, Meetup, Eventbrite (curated) |
| Nonprofits | Local nonprofits by cause | ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer API (free), IRS exempt org file |
| Mentorship | Student programs and clubs | FIU / MDC / UM pages (curated) + synthetic mentees |
| Neighborhood fit | Rent by zip | Zillow Observed Rent Index (free CSV) |
| Transit | Metrorail, Metromover, buses | Miami-Dade Transit GTFS feed; Citi Bike stations |
| Flood risk | Flood zones | FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer |
| Users | Alex + synthetic newcomers and students | Generated (`data/`) |

For the hackathon: a **hand-curated catalog** (`data/quests.json`) beats fighting APIs.
