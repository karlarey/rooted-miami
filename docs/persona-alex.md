# Primary persona: Alex, the solo office engineer

**Alex, 28, software engineer**
- Moved from **NYC** ~1 year ago for an **in-office** job (Brickell / Wynwood)
- Came **alone**; no family or friends in Miami
- Drives after 6 years of subway; ~1 hr/day in traffic
- Social life runs almost entirely through coworkers

**Week:** work, gym, takeout, Thursday coworker drinks. Weekends feel empty unless the team plans something.

## Pain points
1. **Life runs through one employer:** lose the job, lose the community.
2. **No lasting community outside work:** events give acquaintances, nothing repeats.
3. **Transportation shock:** car dependence kills spontaneous plans.
4. **Hard to adjust:** wrong neighborhood, unfamiliar culture, no way *in*.
5. **Cost shock:** rent, car, insurance, flood risk.
6. **No purpose here:** "I live here, but I'm not part of anything."

## Moments when Alex might leave
- Layoff or job change
- Close coworkers leave
- Lease renewal with a rent jump
- Year 1–2 reflection: "What am I building here?"

## What would make Alex stay
- A **community that outlasts its people:** rituals and an institution, not a friend group
- A **purpose bigger than circumstances:** students and causes that need them
- An **identity:** "I'm a Miamian"

**The test:** *If Alex lost their job tomorrow, would they look for the next one in Miami or in NYC?*
