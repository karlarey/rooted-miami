# Alex's first 12 months with Rooted

## 0. First touch
HR includes Rooted in the relocation package, *"Your Miami welcome package,"* or a coworker texts Alex El Pana's number.
**Feeling:** *Someone expected me.*

## 1. Day 1: welcome (character creation)
> **El Pana:** "¡Bienvenido a Miami, Alex! I'm El Pana, your buddy in the city. Let's make Miami feel like home. A few quick questions…"

3-minute conversation: where from, where you live and work, car or not, what you loved back home, what you miss most, causes you care about, weekend time.
**Output:** the welcome package: personal Miami map, first 3 side quests, level 🌱 Seed.

## 2. Week 1: first side quests (Seed)
- 🧭 Ride the Metromover loop (free, best tour of downtown)
- ☕ Find your cafecito spot near home
- 🎾 Saturday tennis ladder at Crandon Park: "3 NYC engineers play. Want in?"

## 3. Month 1: finding your people (→ 🌿 Sprout)
Recurring ritual forms; El Pana makes intros ("Maria also moved from NYC and joined the run club"); Alex joins a newcomer cohort.

## 4. Months 2–3: being needed
> **El Pana:** "Diego is a junior at MDC studying CS, first-gen, wants to break into backend. 2 hours a month. Want to meet him?"

First coffee → monthly sessions → résumé → mock interview. Or a nonprofit quest.

## 5. Risk moments: El Pana steps in first
| Trigger | El Pana |
|---|---|
| 3 weeks quiet | "Missed you at tennis. Easy beach volleyball Sunday?" |
| Lease renewal in 60 days | "Want to compare neighborhoods near your tennis group and on the Metrorail?" |
| Close coworker leaves | "Your Thursday crew is still here, and there's a newcomer dinner Friday." |
| **Layoff** | "Sorry, Alex. 4 Miami companies hire for your stack. Two people in your mentor cohort can refer you. Diego's rooting for you." |

**The climax:** Alex gets laid off and looks for the next job **in Miami**, because their life here doesn't depend on that job.

## 6. Month 6: 🌳 Rooted
Intent to stay 4 → 8. Diego lands an internship. *"You're officially Rooted. Welcome home, Miamian."* Real-world Rooted gathering.

## 7. Month 12: 🌲 Grove
Alex becomes a Host: welcomes newcomers, starts a pickleball group, writes side quests. Diego graduates, stays, becomes a mentor. Year-in-review.
