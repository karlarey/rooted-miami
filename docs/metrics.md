# Impact metrics

```
Communities embraced → created ──► Life satisfaction ↑
                                         │
                                         ▼
                    Miami growth ◄── Retention ↑
```

Community is the **input**, satisfaction the **feeling**, retention the **outcome**, Miami growth the **payoff**.

## 1. Retention (north star)
| Measure | How |
|---|---|
| Intent to stay (0–10) | Check-in at months 0, 3, 6, 12 |
| Actual retention | Still in Miami at 12 / 24 months |
| Weathered a shock | Layoff / rent jump / friends left, and stayed |
| Student retention | Mentees still in Miami after graduating |

## 2. Life satisfaction
| Measure | How |
|---|---|
| Life satisfaction | Cantril Ladder (Gallup, 0–10) |
| Belonging | Sense of Community Index (SCI-2) |
| Connection | People outside work you can count on |
| Identity | "I'm a Miamian": Yes / Not yet / No |

## 3. Miami growth
| Measure | How |
|---|---|
| Talent kept | Engineers + students retained |
| Mentee outcomes | Internships / jobs landed in Miami |
| Volunteer impact | Hours × Independent Sector value per volunteer hour |
| Builders | Startups founded, local hires by members |

## 4. Communities embraced and created
| Measure | How |
|---|---|
| Embraced | Communities joined |
| Ritual consistency | Months of recurring attendance |
| Created | New communities started by members (guest → host) |
| Welcomed | Newcomers welcomed by members |

## Monthly check-in (5 questions, asked by El Pana)
1. How likely are you to live in Miami 2 years from now? (0–10)
2. Best possible life = 10, worst = 0: where are you today? (Cantril)
3. How many people outside work could you call for help?
4. Do you feel part of a community here? (1–5)
5. Do you consider yourself a Miamian? (Yes / Not yet / No)
